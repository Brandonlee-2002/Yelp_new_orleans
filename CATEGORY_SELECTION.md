## Selection requirements

- Choose at least three related restaurant categories.
- Use category counts and category-pair co-occurrence evidence.
- Include at least 10 restaurants.
- Include restaurants in at least 7 geographic areas under the selected geographic system.
- Use an explicit inclusion rule, such as matching at least one selected category.
- Explain why the categories form one coherent theme rather than simply choosing the most common labels.

- Number of geographic areas represented
- Minimum and maximum restaurant counts by area
- Whether the category set is broad enough for stable comparisons
