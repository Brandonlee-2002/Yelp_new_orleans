# Decision-oriented summary

## If the goal is the strongest individual restaurant

Start with the top-15 shortlist, led by Tito's Ceviche & Pisco, Ajun Cajun, and Restaurant August. Read each score with its review and reviewer counts; the ranks are not confidence-certified quality estimates.

## If the goal is the widest selection

Ward 4 contains the largest full theme pool, with 116 restaurants. This answers “where are there the most options?” rather than “where is the adjusted average highest?”

## If the goal is the highest adjusted area score

Ward 9 leads under the primary rules with 12 eligible restaurants and a mean adjusted score of +0.083. The winner changes under several reasonable alternatives, so the recommendation is conditional.

## What a production version would add

Current operation, price, travel time, accessibility, recency, and user preferences are not modeled. A decision-support product should add those dimensions and let a user choose the tradeoff instead of presenting one universal winner.
